# Limitations, Epistemic Scope, and Negative Findings (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document explicitly states what `catalytic-register-frontiers` **does not** prove, documents negative computational findings, and classifies discovered defects using the taxonomy `{bug, error, issue, problem, dilemma, conflict}`.

## 1. Explicit Scope Boundaries

1. **Prime fields vs. extension fields (Theorems T1 & T1e):**
   Theorem `T1` (`rp_pass_two_iff`) holds unconditionally for every **prime field** $\mathbb{F}_p$. It does **not** hold verbatim for non-prime finite fields $\mathbb{F}_{p^e}$ ($e \ge 2$): Theorem `T1e` (`ExtensionFieldSeparation.lean`) now formally proves in Lean 4.35 that over $\mathbb{F}_4$, $x^5 \in \mathrm{RP}_{\mathbb{F}_4}^{\mathrm{pass}}(2, 3)$ and $x^3 \in \mathrm{RP}_{\mathbb{F}_4}^{\mathrm{pass}}(2, 4)$ while $x^7 \notin \mathrm{RP}_{\mathbb{F}_4}^{\mathrm{pass}}(2, t)$ for all $t$. A general closed-form criterion for arbitrary $\mathbb{F}_{p^e}$ remains open (`O3`).
2. **Formal polynomial cleanliness vs. functional evaluation:**
   Cleanliness in Definition 2.2 is formal polynomial identity in $R[O, R_1, \dots, R_{r-1}, x]$ (equivalently, clean evaluation over every commutative $R$-algebra). If one only required pointwise agreement as functions $\mathbb{F}_p \to \mathbb{F}_p$, then $x^p \equiv x$ functionally and degree $> p - 1$ would collapse.
3. **Finite family ($t \in \{5, 6, 7, 8, 9\}$) vs. all $t \ge 4$ (Theorem T3):**
   Our Lean 4.35 proofs establish all five explicit programs $x^7 \in \mathrm{RP}_K^{\mathrm{pass}}(3, 5)$ (`T3a`), $x^9 \in \mathrm{RP}_K^{\mathrm{pass}}(3, 6)$ (`T3b`), $x^{11} \in \mathrm{RP}_K^{\mathrm{pass}}(3, 7)$ (`T3c`), $x^{13} \in \mathrm{RP}_K^{\mathrm{pass}}(3, 8)$ (`T3d`), and $x^{15} \in \mathrm{RP}_K^{\mathrm{pass}}(3, 9)$ (`T3g`), plus unconditional global integer optimality of $t = 8$ for all $t \ge 4$ (`T3f`). Proving $D_{3,\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{pass}}(t) = 2t - 3$ for all $t \ge 10$ (and matching degree upper bounds) remains open (`O1`).
4. **Bad-prime & finite-characteristic restrictions for $t = 6, 7, 8$ and `T3h`:**
   While `prog5_x7` ($\epsilon = 0.827087$) avoids only $\{2, 3\}$ (matching Vinciguerra's $t = 4$ characteristic requirement!), `prog6_x9` avoids $\{2, 3, 5, 17\}$, and `prog7_x11` / `prog8_x13` avoid two larger primes arising from clearing denominators (`538423, 2512452073` and `137, 1602207194491`). Furthermore, in 4-register passive powering (`T3h`, Corollary 5.2), inverting the digit-program and Cook–Mertz finite-difference denominators $(\delta - 1)!$ and $(\lfloor\log_\delta N\rfloor + 1)!$ requires characteristic $0$ or prime characteristic $p > \max(12, \lfloor\log_{13} N\rfloor + 1)$ with $p \nmid D_8$ (for `prog8_x13`) / $p > \max(6, \lfloor\log_7 N\rfloor + 1)$ (for `prog5_x7`). All constructions hold unconditionally in characteristic $0$.
5. **No $r = 4$ exponent improvement:**
   Our exploratory exact modular search over $r = 4$ triangular closed loops (`deg_v = 2, 3, 4`, `wt(w) = 3..9`, unrestricted monomials) reached $N = 7$ ($t=4$), $N = 9$ ($t=5$, $\epsilon = 0.73249$), and $N = 11$ ($t=6$, $\epsilon = 0.74722$). Because degree grew as $2t - 1$ rather than faster, this family did **not** beat Vinciguerra's $r = 4$ exponent $\log_7 4 \approx 0.71241$.
6. **Boyar–Find conjecture & SAT certificate boundary:**
   `exp4_boolean_mc_companion.py` provides supporting SAT-certified evidence (0 counterexamples across sampled degree-3 cancellations and hard $n = 4$ maps) and does **not** prove or refute Boyar–Find (TCS 720, 2018) or Morse's $n = 5$ conjecture (`arXiv:2608.30238`). Our CNF encoder is verified against an independent exhaustive brute-force enumerator on all 16 $n=2$ functions ($k=0, 1$) and canonical $n=3$ benchmarks in `tests/test_all.py`, AND all **8/8** CaDiCaL 2.1.2 DIMACS `.cnf` + ASCII `.lrat` UNSAT certificates (`results/sat_certs/`, 18,932 total verified steps) are archived and independently verified step-by-step by `scripts/verify_lrat.py` (including mutant-rejection tests in `tests/test_all.py`). They have **not** yet been replayed through CakeML's formally verified `cake_lpr` binary (external binary not installed locally — classified as `problem`; no Tier-1 Lean theorem depends on SAT).
7. **Peer-review, Windows tooling, & archival provenance status:**
   This manuscript has **not** undergone external peer review or external second-machine human reproduction (`REPLICATION.md`). On Windows, `lean4lean.exe` bundled in Lean 4.35.0 fails locally with an upstream `.olean` header-version mismatch (`incompatible header`, classified as `problem`), and `lake check --paranoid` requires POSIX `bwrap`; accordingly, `lean4lean` is omitted from the completed local kernel count (`results/six_kernel_audit.txt`). Correctness of all Tier-1 theorems is certified locally across **5 distinct Lean 4.35 kernel implementations in 6 runs** (`lean`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche --verified`, `con-ron --verified`, `nanoda_bin 0.4.19`). **Confirmed live archival identifiers:** GitHub (`0thernes/catalytic-register-frontiers`, live CI & SLSA releases `v1.0.0`/`v1.0.1`), GitHub Pages Blueprint (`https://0thernes.github.io/catalytic-register-frontiers/`), Zenodo Concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24517198` (`v1.0.1` Version DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24517199`), Software Heritage snapshot `swh:1:snp:f960eeda6f145e54c0ac52538ace866a16e24085`, ORCID `0009-0006-8168-9648`, and `0thernes-research-archive` record `0TH-2026-010`. **Planned deposits not yet assigned:** Palomar Registry ID (under submission), arXiv ID, HAL ID, and Bitcoin block confirmation for `provenance/MANIFEST.sha256.ots` (`ROADMAP.md`).
