In **Pass 21**, every overclaiming docstring across `Challenge.lean`, `PalomarChallenge.lean`, `DigitSumDichotomy.lean`, `ThreeRegisterBreakthrough.lean`, and `FourRegisterPowering.lean` was corrected (comment-only 1-for-1 edits preserving all theorem types, proofs, and line counts).
