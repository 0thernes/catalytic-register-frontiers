Abstract:
Recent operator analyses of passive-output catalytic register programs by Vinciguerra (arXiv:2609.18692) established that over characteristic-zero fields, every two-register passive program computing a degree-d >= 2 polynomial requires at least d + 1 input accesses, while four-access r-register programs achieve degree 2r - 1 (yielding a three-register base exponent log_5 4 approx 0.86135 for powering, streaming frequency moments, and matrix powering). We resolve two open structural questions left by that framework and formalize all main theorems from first principles in zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0. First, we prove an exact base-p digit-sum dichotomy over every prime field F_p: a univariate polynomial f(x) lies in RP^{pass}_{F_p}(2, t) if and only if every term x^d (d >= 2) with nonzero coefficient satisfies s_p(d) <= min(p - 1, t - 1). This simultaneously establishes unbounded-degree two-access Frobenius collapse (x^{p^m} in RP^{pass}_{F_p}(2, 2)), absolute saturation when s_p(d) >= p (e.g., x^3 not in RP^{pass}_{F_2}(2, t) for all t), sharp F_4 extension-field separation, and exact recovery of Vinciguerra's bound when d < p. Second, we show the two-access characteristic-2 collapse persists under strictly bilinear and distinct-operand updates (x^{2^k} in k + 1 or k + 2 registers and 2 input accesses). Third, we construct explicit three-register passive programs computing x^7, x^9, x^{11}, x^{13}, and x^{15} in 5..9 accesses, strictly reducing the three-register exponent to the unique global integer minimizer log_{13} 8 approx 0.81071 for powering and catalytic streaming (and log_7 5 approx 0.82709 at the exact r = 3 scalar register tier for non-commutative matrix powering). Fourth, we formalize a zero-workspace balanced bilinear product in log_2 d + 2 registers and (7d^2 - 4)/3 ops, and contrast catalytic nonlinear cancellation against Boolean multiplicative complexity (arXiv:2608.30238).
