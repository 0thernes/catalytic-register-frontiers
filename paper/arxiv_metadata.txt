Abstract:
We prove new results extending Vinciguerra's operator analysis of passive-output catalytic register programs (arXiv:2609.18692); no question posed in that paper is resolved, and all main theorems are formalized from first principles in zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0. First, under formal-polynomial cleanliness with program constants in a prime field F_p, we prove a base-p digit-sum dichotomy: a univariate polynomial f(x) lies in RP^{pass}_{F_p}(2, t) if and only if every term x^d (d >= 2) with nonzero coefficient satisfies s_p(d) <= min(p - 1, t - 1). Thus characteristic-0 lower bounds do not extend to char. p (x^{p^m} in 2 accesses), formal polynomials with s_p(d) >= p (e.g., x^3 over F_2, though x^3 = x pointwise on F_2) have no F_p-constant program for any t, F_4 exhibits sharp extension-field separation, and d < p gives a large-characteristic analogue of Theorem 3.5. Second, we show 2-access characteristic-2 programs persist under bilinear and distinct-operand updates (x^{2^k} in k + 1 or k + 2 registers). Third, explicit three-register Pascal-row shear programs compute x^7, x^9, x^{11}, x^{13}, and x^{15} in 5..9 accesses, improving the four-register powering exponent bound from log_5 4 approx 0.86135 to log_{13} 8 approx 0.81071 (smallest among our constructed programs and unique integer minimizer of log_{2t-3} t for t >= 4, not proved optimal among all three-register programs), and tightening paper-level streaming and non-commutative matrix powering (log_7 5 approx 0.82709 via prog5_x7). Fourth, we formalize the classical four-step balanced zero-workspace bilinear product (cf. Ben-Or and Cleve 1992) in log_2 d + 2 registers and (7d^2 - 4)/3 ops, and contrast catalytic cancellation against Boolean multiplicative complexity (arXiv:2608.30238).
