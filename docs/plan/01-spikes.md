# Feasibility Spikes & Measurements (`docs/plan/01-spikes.md`)

> Pipeline artifact `P1.F2`. Records the exact exploratory computational spikes (`probe1`–`probe12` during problem discovery) and their measured outcomes before formalizing in Lean 4.35.0.

| Spike | Question Tested | Measured Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — Prime-Field Two-Register Sweep** | Does $x^d \in \mathrm{RP}_{\mathbb{F}_p}^{\mathrm{pass}}(2, t)$ depend only on $s_p(d)$? | Exact RREF over $\mathbb{F}_p$ ($p \in \{2,3,5,7\}$) matched $s_p(d) \le \min(p-1, t-1)$ across 100% of tested degrees (107 feasible, 82 exhaustive subset lower-bound audits, 349 impossible; 0 exceptions). | **Go** → Formalized unconditionally as Theorem T1 (`DigitSumDichotomy.lean`). |
| **S2 — Extension Field $\mathbb{F}_4$ Counter-Check** | Does the digit-sum characterization extend verbatim to $\mathbb{F}_{p^e}$ ($e \ge 2$)? | No: over $\mathbb{F}_4$, $x^5$ is computable in $t=3$ (not $t=2$) and $x^3$ in $t=4$ (not $t=3$), while $x^7$ is impossible for all $t$. | **Scope Lock + Formalize** → Restrict T1 strictly to prime fields $\mathbb{F}_p$; formalize sharp $\mathbb{F}_4$ separation as Tier-1 `T1e` (`ExtensionFieldSeparation.lean`). |
| **S3 — Three-Register $t = 5$ `deg_w = 3` vs `deg_w = 2`** | Can 3 passive catalytic registers beat Vinciguerra's $t=4, x^5$ exponent $\log_5 4 \approx 0.86135$? | `deg_w = 3` failed to reach $x^7$ at $t=5$ (classified `problem`); switching to `deg_w = 2` immediately unlocked $x^7$ with $D = 576$, bad primes $\{2,3\}$ only, $\epsilon = \log_7 5 \approx 0.82709$! | **Double Down** → Extend schedule to $t=6, 7, 8, 9$. |
| **S4 — Optimal $r = 3$ Exponent Minimization** | Where does $\epsilon(t) = \log_{2t-3} t$ attain its integer minimum? | Exact construction succeeds for $t=5$ ($x^7$), $t=6$ ($x^9$), $t=7$ ($x^{11}$), $t=8$ ($x^{13}$, $\epsilon \approx 0.81071$), $t=9$ ($x^{15}$, $\epsilon \approx 0.811368 > \epsilon(8)$). Unique integer minimizer is $t = 8$. | **Go** → Formalize $t \in \{5,6,7,8,9\}$, universal `T3f`, and complete 4-register powering `T3h` (`ThreeRegisterBreakthrough.lean`, `FourRegisterPowering.lean`). |
| **S5 — Four-Register $r = 4$ Triangular Loops** | Can the same triangular nonlinear-cancellation template beat Vinciguerra's $r = 4$ exponent $\log_7 4 \approx 0.71241$? | Reached $x^7$ ($t=4$), $x^9$ ($t=5$, $\epsilon = 0.73249$), $x^{11}$ ($t=6$, $\epsilon = 0.74722$); did **not** beat $0.71241$. | **Kill / Report Negative** → Parked in `docs/plan/ideas.md` and disclosed in `LIMITATIONS.md` & paper Section 5 (Remark 5.5) / Section 8 (Open Problem 8.2). |
| **S6 — Lean Kernel `rfl` Performance for `prog8_x13`** | Can `exec3Poly` evaluate `prog8_x13` purely by kernel reduction (zero `native_decide`) across 6 external checkers? | Initial well-founded `Poly3.add` blocked `rfl` (`bug`); rewriting via nested structural recursion (`Poly3.addAux`) enabled pure `rfl` checking in < 10 s on `lean`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, and `nanoda_bin`. | **Go** → Zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35 confirmed feasible (ADR 0001). |
