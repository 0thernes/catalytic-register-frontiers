# Machine-Auditable Claims Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every scientific claim in `catalytic-register-frontiers` is assigned an explicit epistemic tier and an explicit refutation condition (Build Standard B04, B42):

- **`TIER-1 (Lean 4.35 Multi-Kernel)`**: Formally verified from first principles in `lean/Solution.lean` + `lean/Catalytic/NonVacuity.lean` (zero Mathlib, zero forbidden tokens, strict core linters clean, axioms $\subseteq \{\text{propext}, \text{Classical.choice}, \text{Quot.sound}\}$, 6 passing kernel runs across 5 implementations).
- **`TIER-2 (Exact-Arithmetic / SAT-Certified)`**: 100% exact integer, rational (`fractions.Fraction`), finite-field ($\mathbb{F}_p$, $\mathbb{F}_4$), or CaDiCaL 2.1.2 SAT/UNSAT computation verified by `pytest`, Hypothesis property tests, mutant checks, and brute-force SAT encoder equivalence tests.
- **`TIER-3 (Paper-Level Reduction)`**: Standard mathematical deduction combining our Tier-1 theorems (`T3a`, `T3d`, `T3h`, `T3i`) with published reductions in Vinciguerra (`arXiv:2609.18692`, Corollary 5.1.1 bit-space accounting, §4.4, and Theorem 5.2).
- **`TIER-4 (Open Frontier)`**: Explicitly unresolved conjecture or research question.

## Tier-1 Machine-Checked Lean 4.35 Theorems

Scope notes (2026-11-26 statement audit, [log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)): in `T1`–`T1e`, "cleanly computes" means equality of formal polynomials in $\mathbb{F}_p[R, x]$ (or $\mathbb{F}_4[R, x]$) with program constants in that field. As functions on the finite field the statements do not apply, since $x^p = x$. `T1s` and `T1es` prove that formal cleanliness implies correct execution over every commutative ring of characteristic $p$ (every commutative $\mathbb{F}_4$-algebra); the converse is not a Lean theorem, so the impossibility results concern the symbolic semantics. No upper bound for three-register programs is proved, and no three-register exponent is claimed optimal. No question posed in Vinciguerra (`arXiv:2609.18692`) is resolved.

## Tier-3 Paper-Level Reductions

- **`R1` (Now Tier-1 `T3h`):** Four-register passive powering for all $N \ge 1$ (`Cor. 5.2`), over rings where the stated constants are invertible, is machine-checked in Lean 4.35 (`lean/Catalytic/FourRegisterPowering.lean`, `T3h_FourRegPowering`) across 5 distinct kernel implementations (6 runs). The real-exponent and big-O forms are paper-level conversions of the integer bound.
- **`R2` (Catalytic streaming frequency moments $F_D(\sigma)$ — coordinatewise powering bound in Tier-1 `T3i`; stream model and bit-space accounting Tier-3):** The algebraic vector powering bound ($F_D(\mathbf{f}) = \sum_{i<n} f_i^D$ cleanly computed over the product ring `Fin n → R` in $< \frac{104}{3} \cdot 8^\ell \le \frac{8}{3} \cdot 13 D^{\log_{13} 8}$ passes for every $n$ and $D \ge 1$) is now **machine-checked in Lean 4.35** (`T3i_StreamingFrequencyMoment`, `FourRegisterPowering.lean`). The remaining bit-space accounting over $\mathbb{F}_p^n$ with $p > \max(12, m^D, \lfloor\log_{13} D\rfloor + 1)$ and $p \nmid D_8$ — $O(n D \log(m+1))$ catalytic bits and $O(D \log(m+1) + \log n + \log D)$ regular workspace bits (matching Corollary 5.1.1 of `arXiv:2609.18692`) — stays Tier-3.
- **`R3` (Non-commutative matrix powering $A^N$):** Lifting `T3a` (`prog5_x7`, shear degree $d = 2$) via Vinciguerra §4.4's $\ell_A(z)$ homogeneous lift cleanly computes $A^N$ for $A \in M_m(\mathbb{F}_p)$ ($p > \max(7, \lfloor \log_7 N \rfloor + 1)$) using at most $\frac{10}{3} \cdot 7 \cdot N^{\log_7 5} = O(N^{0.82709})$ recursive matrix calls and the **exact** $r=3$ scalar register count $2m^2 + \binom{m^2+2}{2} - 1 = 3m^2 + \binom{m^2+1}{2}$ (zero extra scalar registers vs. Theorem 5.2 of `arXiv:2609.18692`). Lifting `T3d` (`prog8_x13`, shear degree $d = 6$, $p > \max(13, \lfloor \log_{13} N \rfloor + 1)$, $p \nmid D_8$) achieves $O(N^{0.81072})$ calls using $3m^2 + \binom{m^2+5}{6} = \Theta(m^{12})$ scalar registers. (Concrete non-commutative instances — literal $\mu_7(\texttt{prog5\_x7})$ on $M_2(\mathbb{F}_{11})$ with non-commuting catalytic $(O_0, u_0(z), v_0(z))$, single-catalyst $\mu_2$-lifted and 2-factor Cook–Mertz $A^{23}$ on $M_2(\mathbb{F}_{11})$ / $M_3(\mathbb{F}_{11})$, and the scalar-register identity for $m = 1..20$ — are independently verified in exact modular arithmetic by `test_pass9_cor54_matrix_lift_and_blueprint_integrity` in `tests/test_all.py`; the general symbolic matrix-lift theorem stays Tier-3.)

## What Would Prove Us Wrong (Refutation & Falsification Criteria — Build Standard B42)

Every claim in this repository is explicitly falsifiable:

1. **Refuting Formal Correctness (`T1`–`T4`, `NONVAC`):** Any of: (a) a clean rebuild of `lean/` failing or emitting any axiom outside `{propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`; (b) any of the 5 distinct kernel implementations across 6 runs (`lean`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, `nanoda_bin`) rejecting the exported proof stream; or (c) any mismatch between `lean/Challenge.lean` definitions and Vinciguerra (`arXiv:2609.18692`, Def. 2.2) documented in `docs/statement-fidelity.md`.
2. **Refuting `T1` (Digit-Sum Dichotomy):** Exhibiting a single prime $p$, budget $t \ge 1$, and passive 2-register program with $\mathbb{F}_p$ constants cleanly computing, as an identity of formal polynomials in $\mathbb{F}_p[R, x]$, a monomial $x^d$ ($d \ge 2$) with $s_p(d) > \min(p-1, t-1)$ — e.g., any clean 2-register program computing $x^3$ over $\mathbb{F}_2[x]$ in any number of calls $t$.
3. **Refuting `T2` or `T4`:** Exhibiting an input/catalytic state where `frobBilinProg` (over a `Char2CRing`) or `balancedProdProg` (over a `CRing`) fails to restore a single catalytic register or violates passive/strictly-distinct operand rules, or fails Hypothesis randomized testing in `tests/test_all.py`.
4. **Refuting `T3` (3-Register Exponent Improvement):** Finding a single input/catalyst triple $(x, u, v)$ where `prog5_x7`..`prog8_x13` fails to compute $O + D_t x^{2t-3}$ cleanly, or finding a good prime $p \nmid D_t$ where reduction modulo $p$ fails.
5. **Refuting `E5` (SAT Companion Audit):** Exhibiting a valid 2-AND unrestricted XAG circuit over $\mathbb{F}_2$ computing $\mathbb{F}_4$ multiplication, the orthogonal bent pair, or any of the 6 UNSAT $(n=4, m=3)$ maps — or finding a single input where our CNF encoder disagrees with brute-force ground truth.
6. **Refuting Novelty:** Citing any peer-reviewed paper or preprint predating 2026-11-23 that already proved Theorem `T1` (exact base-$p$ digit-sum IFF for $\mathrm{RP}_{\mathbb{F}_p}^{\mathrm{pass}}(2, t)$) or `T3d` ($x^{13} \in \mathrm{RP}_K^{\mathrm{pass}}(3, 8)$ achieving $\log_{13} 8$). If found, we will immediately log it in `ERRATA.md` and reframe to formalization/rediscovery. `T4` is already classified as a formalisation of a classical construction: Ben-Or and Cleve (SIAM J. Comput. 21(1), 1992, `doi:10.1137/0221006`) compute polynomial-size formulas with three registers in a neighbouring instruction set.

## Tier-4 Open Frontiers (Explicitly Unresolved)

- **`O1`**: Uniform proof of $D_{3,\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{pass}}(t) = 2t - 3$ for all $t \ge 4$ (and matching upper bounds for $r \ge 3$).
- **`O2`**: Whether non-triangular or multi-stage $r = 4$ loops can reach $N \ge 10$ at $t=5$ or $N \ge 13$ at $t=6$ to beat Vinciguerra's $r=4$ exponent $\log_7 4 \approx 0.71241$.
- **`O3`**: General closed-form combinatorial characterization of $\mathrm{RP}_{\mathbb{F}_{p^e}}^{\mathrm{pass}}(2, t)$ over non-prime extension fields ($e \ge 2$).
- **`O4`**: The open question of Boyar and Find (TCS 720, 2018) and Morse's $n = 5$ binary polynomial multiplication program (`arXiv:2608.30238`).
- **`O5`**: Both questions of Vinciguerra (`arXiv:2609.18692`, Sec. 6): whether $D^{\mathrm{pass}}_{r,K}(4) > 2r - 1$, and whether additional input accesses drastically increase $D^{\mathrm{pass}}_{r,K}$. `T3a`–`T3g` give only the lower bounds $D^{\mathrm{pass}}_3(t) \ge 2t - 3$ for $t = 5, \dots, 9$; optimality of any three-register exponent is open.
